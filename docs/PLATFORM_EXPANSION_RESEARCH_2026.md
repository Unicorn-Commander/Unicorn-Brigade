### Implementation Priority

**Tier 1 - Immediate High ROI**:
1. **Help Desk Triage** - Universal need, clear metrics, fast payback
2. **RFP Response** - High-value deals, measurable win rate improvement
3. **Customer Onboarding** - Directly impacts churn and expansion revenue

**Tier 2 - Strategic Value**:
4. **Competitive Intelligence** - Strategic advantage but harder to measure ROI
5. **Code Review & Security** - Critical for tech companies, compliance
6. **Marketing Campaign** - Revenue impact but requires marketing maturity

**Tier 3 - Enterprise/Specialized**:
7. **Contract Lifecycle** - Essential for legal/procurement-heavy orgs
8. **Regulatory Compliance** - Critical for regulated industries
9. **Financial Close** - High value for public companies
10. **Supply Chain Risk** - Manufacturing, retail, logistics focus
11. **Performance Reviews** - HR transformation, culture dependent
12. **Product Launch** - Product-focused companies with frequent launches

### Key Success Patterns

All successful workflows share:
1. **Clear human checkpoints** - AI handles grunt work, humans make final decisions
2. **Measurable outcomes** - Time saved, cost reduced, quality improved
3. **Multi-agent collaboration** - No single agent solves the problem alone
4. **Data integration** - Connects existing systems vs replacing them
5. **Iterative improvement** - Workflows learn from feedback and outcomes
6. **Compliance-aware** - Audit trails, approval workflows, security
