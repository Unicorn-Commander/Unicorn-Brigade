## Orchestration Patterns
1. **Sequential Operations**
   - Legal clearance → Investigation → Action
   - Evidence gathering → Analysis → Prosecution

2. **Parallel Operations**
   - Multiple agents on independent tasks
   - Simultaneous multi-domain actions

3. **Hierarchical Command**
   - Colonel → Domain Leads → Specialist Agents
   - Clear chain of command and communication

4. **Collaborative Networks**
   - Cross-functional teams for complex problems
   - Information sharing across domains
